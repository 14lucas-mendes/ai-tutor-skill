# Review Workflow

Leia `references/learning-strategies.md` antes de escolher retrieval-first ou a política de spacing.

Prioritize low retention, due cards, active weak points, and stale evidence. Apply `retrieval_first` with one short free-recall attempt before restudy, then present one card or task at a time and require an attempt before the answer.

Judge objective correctness first, then ask for confidence. Update spaced-repetition scheduling and retention separately from demonstrated mastery. Use `adaptive_spacing` only when `study-config.json.spacing_policy.mode` is `adaptive`; otherwise preserve the fixed ladder. Use a new context when reconfirming transfer capability.

Periodically test residual capability with a new representative task under `assessment` mode. Do not let generative assistance perform the cognitive core. Conventional documentation remains available when the objective permits it; copied or near-complete solutions do not create autonomous evidence. After the attempt, return to assisted review when useful.

Resolve weak points and close curriculum items only under the normative contracts. Record the review in the active session and validate state.
